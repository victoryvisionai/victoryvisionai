FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Gold $12,500?

Gold combines the full Victory Vision execution platform, including connected email and calling capabilities, advanced automation, relationship intelligence, and bespoke 1-on-1 implementation and training. The price reflects the combination of sophisticated technology and personalized onboarding designed to configure the system around the way you actually win business.

How is Gold different from Starter?

Starter is the learn/practice layer, designed for professionals who want to learn the methodology and begin working their network. Gold includes the full execution stack, including connected email and calling, advanced automation, deeper analytics, and personalized implementation support.

Do I have to book a sales call?

No. Qualified buyers who understand their business-development workflow and recognize the fit can purchase directly from this page.

Is this for teams?

Gold can support a broader workflow, but the core offer is designed around senior rainmakers who remain personally involved in winning business. If you need multi-seat licensing or team workflows, request a conversation to discuss your specific needs.

Does Victory Vision guarantee meetings or revenue?

No. Victory Vision is a business-development operating system that helps you systematize relationship-driven prospecting, outreach, follow-up and pipeline management. Results depend on your market, offer, existing relationships, and execution discipline.

What does the 1-on-1 training include?

Bespoke implementation includes working sessions to define your commercial goal, clarify your ICP and offer, segment your existing network, identify priority targets, configure campaign approach, build outreach workflows, set up calling processes, and establish measurement and next-best-action systems. The scope is designed to get you operational, not provide unlimited ongoing consulting.

What if Gold is more than I need?

If you want to learn the methodology first and begin working your network without the full execution stack, consider starting with Operator's Edge + Victory Vision Starter at $990/year. You can always upgrade to Gold when you are ready for connected email, calling, and advanced automation.
