Origin Story

The Story Behind This

In 2007, I was deployed to Iraq. Between missions, I started cold-calling West Point graduates I'd never met—people who had graduated years before me, people I had no personal connection to. I wasn't asking for favors. I was asking for advice on how to transition from the Army to business school.

Every single person I called took the time to help. One conversation led to another. Those introductions opened doors I didn't know existed. Within a year, I was accepted to Columbia Business School on a full scholarship.

"I didn't have a network. I had an institution. And that institution gave me permission to reach out to people who had no reason to help me—except that we shared something meaningful."

That experience changed everything. It taught me that relationships aren't just about who you know right now. They're about who you could know, who you should know, and having a systematic way to activate those connections when it matters.

Twenty years later, I built Victory Vision because I needed it. And I built it for people like you—professionals who understand that one relationship, properly activated, can change the trajectory of your business.

Your Goal

What's ONE Thing You Want to Make Happen in the Next 90 Days?

Win 3 New Clients

Start with your existing network. Identify 25 people who either fit your ideal client profile or know someone who does. Reach out with a specific, valuable reason to reconnect—not a generic "checking in." Victory Vision helps you prioritize who to contact, research their current situation, and create personalized outreach that gets responses.

Reach PE Portfolio Companies

Private equity firms are relationship-driven. The fastest path in is through a warm introduction or a shared institutional connection. Victory Vision helps you identify which PE professionals you're connected to (directly or indirectly), track portfolio company changes, and create targeted campaigns that position you as a valuable resource to their portfolio.

Find a Referral Partner

The best referral partners serve the same clients you do but offer complementary services. Map your existing clients and identify patterns—who else is in their ecosystem? Victory Vision helps you systematically identify potential partners, track interactions, and build relationships that generate consistent, high-quality referrals.

Enter a New Vertical

Breaking into a new industry requires credibility and introductions. Start by identifying 10 people in your network who either work in that vertical or have strong connections there. Victory Vision helps you research the vertical, identify key players, and create a systematic outreach plan that positions you as someone worth talking to.
