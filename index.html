01 · Autonomous AI SDR

Sources the right owners. Writes the right first line.

The analyst in your ear during every seller call.

Listens live to your call. Pulls the contact’s customer journey, priorities, and close signals. Surfaces 1-sentence tactical nudges during the conversation — “they just mentioned a 2027 liquidity event; ask about timing” — so the tenth call of the day feels like the first.
