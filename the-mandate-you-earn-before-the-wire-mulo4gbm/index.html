Tactical Framework

The Twelve-Conversation Framework

A concrete structure for systematic cultivation that converts dormant connections into committed allocations. Each conversation builds trust and deepens alignment without pitching prematurely.

Conversations 1–3

Reconnect without agenda. Share market perspective. Establish you're thinking about the same problems.

Conversations 4–6

Add value asymmetrically. Introduce a useful contact. Share non-obvious research. Demonstrate you understand their mandate.

Conversations 7–9

Deepen strategic alignment. Discuss thesis evolution. Explore co-investment appetite. Position your pipeline without pitching.

Conversations 10–12

Earn the mandate. When the right opportunity surfaces, you're the first call because trust has already been built.
