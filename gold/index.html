Why is Gold $12,500?

How is Gold different from Starter?

Starter is the learn/practice layer designed for professionals who want to understand the methodology first. Gold includes the full execution stack, including connected email and calling capabilities, advanced automation, deeper analytics, and personalized implementation support that are not included in Starter.

Do I have to book a sales call?

No. Qualified buyers who understand their needs and are ready to proceed can purchase directly. However, if you would like to discuss your specific workflow and ensure Gold is the right fit, we welcome that conversation.

Is this for teams?

Gold can support a broader workflow and team collaboration, but the core offer is designed around senior rainmakers who remain personally involved in winning business. If you are looking to delegate the entire process to junior staff, Gold may not be the right fit.

Does Victory Vision guarantee meetings or revenue?

No. Victory Vision is a business-development operating system that helps you execute more systematically. Results depend on your market, offer, relationships, execution and many other factors we cannot control. We do not make guarantees about meetings, pipeline or revenue.

What does the 1-on-1 training include?

Bespoke implementation includes working sessions to define your commercial goal, clarify your ICP and offer, segment your network, configure your first campaigns, set up outreach and calling workflows, and establish your measurement process. The exact scope is tailored to your business model and needs.
