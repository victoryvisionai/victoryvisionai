No. It combines curriculum, community, live teaching, office hours, accountability, and hands-on platform use. The focus is on building a complete business-development system, not just learning about AI.

Do I need to speak with someone before joining?

No. Membership is self-serve. You can join immediately and start accessing content, community, and Victory Vision Starter right away.

No. It is for relationship-driven professionals whose business depends on clients, investors, candidates, referrals, partnerships, or deals. This includes founders, executives, recruiters, advisers, consultants, investors, and other professionals where relationships drive revenue.

No. The program provides methodology, tools, accountability, and technology to help improve business-development execution. Results depend on your market, offer, effort, and execution quality.

Operator's Edge teaches you how to build the engine. Victory Vision helps you run the engine every day. This package includes both the education layer (Operator's Edge) and the Starter tier of the platform (Victory Vision).

Can I upgrade from Starter later?

Yes. Victory Vision Starter is designed to help you learn and prove the system. As your needs grow, you can upgrade to more advanced Victory Vision tiers with additional automation, capacity, and features.
